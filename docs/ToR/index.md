WGMLEARN's work is organized around three Terms of Reference. Together, they identify marine machine-learning challenges, strengthen collaboration across the ICES community, and build practical capacity through shared events and resources.

## Current Terms of Reference

### [ToR A: Identify unmet challenges]({{ '/ToR/unmet-challenges/' | relative_url }})

Develop a list of relevant AI and ML topics and tasks, emphasizing unmet marine-domain challenges and their potential benefits. This work integrates output from WKAAII and links to relevant ICES working groups, research groups, and public datasets.

### [ToR B: Coordinate ICES working groups]({{ '/ToR/coordinate-working-groups/' | relative_url }})

Establish contact with other ICES working groups to explore collaboration opportunities, chart research and knowledge needs, and support strategic AI development within ICES.

### [ToR C: Organize events]({{ '/ToR/organize-events/' | relative_url }})

Organize tutorials, hackathons, workshops, and conference sessions that address important challenges and build capacity across the marine science community.
